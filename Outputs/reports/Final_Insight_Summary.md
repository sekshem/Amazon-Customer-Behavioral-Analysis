# Amazon Customer Behavioral Analysis — Final Insight Summary

## Dataset Overview
- **Rows:** 602 respondents (600 after cleaning)
- **Columns:** 23 features covering demographics, purchase patterns, satisfaction, and preferences

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Shopping Frequency
| Frequency | Count | % |
|---|---|---|
| Few times a month | 203 | 34% |
| Less than once a month | 124 | 21% |
| Once a week | 112 | 19% |
| Once a month | 107 | 18% |
| Multiple times a week | 56 | 9% |

**Insight:** The bulk of customers are moderate-frequency shoppers. Only 9% are power buyers.

### 2. Cart Abandonment (Payment/Checkout Friction)
- **42%** — Found a better price elsewhere
- **40%** — Changed mind / no longer needed
- **12%** — High shipping costs
- **6%** — Other reasons

**Insight:** Price competitiveness and intent persistence are the two biggest checkout levers.

### 3. Age Distribution
- Mean age: **30.8 years**, Std Dev: 10.2
- Core audience: **18–35 year-olds** (highest purchase frequency)
- 45+ segment: deliberate buyers with narrower satisfaction variance

### 4. Gender Patterns
- **Female:** 58% of respondents; dominate Beauty, Fashion
- **Male:** 24%; broader category spread; Home & Kitchen
- **Prefer not to say:** 15%

### 5. Satisfaction
- Overall satisfaction is low — median score of **2/5**
- No age group is notably more satisfied
- Root cause: likely fulfilment/product-quality issues, not demographics

## Top 5 Marketing Recommendations

1. **Flash Price Match alerts** to recover the 42% who leave for cheaper competitors
2. **Cart abandonment drip campaign** (24hr email + 5% coupon)
3. **Beauty Box subscription** targeting female 18–35 Power Buyers
4. **Prime benefits promotion** for Occasional Buyers to overcome shipping cost barrier
5. **Review incentive program** to convert the 48% who have never left a review
